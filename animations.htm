Detector effects on track reconstruction for three benchmark performances
The effect of three chosen resolution performances on an initial recoil track pointing along the positive z-axis is shown. The colourbar illustrates the angular separation between the smeared directions (here shown as 1000 dots) and the original one, with darker colour signifying larger separation.

Demonstration of Kuiper Test
Left: 1000 recoil events generated from a coherent elastic neutrino neutron scattering distribution shown as dots and rotated by different angles θ and Φ on a sphere.
Centre: The Φ distribution calculated with respect to each θ, Φ test axis.
Right: The empirical CDF of the Φ data, as well as the uniform CDF. As the CEνNS cross section assumes azimuthal symmetry, the eCDF aligns with the uniform one when the correct rotational axis is found, minimising the Kuiper statistic, V.
